Bimodal Commute Curves

Urban interstate flow exhibits two steep, deterministic surges at 7-9 AM and 4-6 PM. Overnight volumes (1-4 AM) drop by over 88%.

Weather Sensitivity

Precipitation and freezing temperatures contract highway capacity by 15% to 35%. Rain and snow force longer vehicle headway and slower speeds.

Cyclical Encoding Power

Mapping hours to sine and cosine waves ($\sin(2\pi t/24), \cos(2\pi t/24)$) guarantees mathematical continuity across the 23:00 to 00:00 midnight threshold.

Adaptive Signal Control

Deploying predictive on-ramp metering 15 minutes before peak buildup prevents freeway breakdown and shaves up to 20% off commuter travel delays.
